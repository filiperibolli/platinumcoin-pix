D · the cost of a replay — 16 threads × 20 replays of one already-committed txId
====================================================================================================
Step 50 noted that a replay under the pessimistic strategy pays for a row lock before
it discovers it is a replay. This measures that, against the optimistic strategy which
takes no lock — and against the same strategies committing NEW postings, as the scale.
